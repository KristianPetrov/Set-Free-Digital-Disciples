## Full-logo generation prompt

Create ONE refined new logo for SET FREE DIGITAL DISCIPLES based closely on the two supplied older logo designs. Asset: premium futuristic cyberpunk Christian digital studio brand logo, genuine transparent background, square composition. Preserve the recognizable bearded Jesus figure with long hair, futuristic visor, and luminous cyan circular halo from reference 1; borrow subtle purple circuit accents and the confident upward finger gesture of reference 2. Make the figure an elegant sharp graphic emblem with clean strong silhouette, obsidian metallic surfaces, carefully drawn cyan circuitry and restrained neon rim light, not photorealistic, not excessively detailed. A bold distinctive custom condensed futuristic wordmark arcs minimally across the top reading EXACTLY 'SET FREE'. At bottom two strong centered lines reading EXACTLY 'DIGITAL' and 'DISCIPLES', deep luminous red with clean precise lettering. All three lines clear and correctly spelled, with clean typography stronger than the old references. Subject and text in cohesive compact shield-shaped badge silhouette. Make the central visor/face emblem recognizable at small sizes. More polished, powerful and contemporary while obviously belonging to the existing identity. Transparency outside the figure and text, NO gray/black rectangular background, NO square glow, NO frame, NO additional text, NO watermarks. Keep glow subtle and tight, plenty of transparent margin around all sides, don't cut off any letters or halo. Return a single finished brand logo, no mockups or alternatives.

References: SetFreeDigitalDisciplesMatrix.png and SetFreeDigitalDisciplesPortal.png.

## Compact-mark generation prompt

Asset type: favicon and compact navbar emblem derived from supplied new Set Free Digital Disciples logo. Use supplied image as exact identity reference. Create a SINGLE compact square emblem of ONLY the SAME bearded Jesus head with long hair, SAME luminous futuristic cyan visor and circular cyan halo. Keep recognizable angular metallic dark face, subtly purple circuitry. This is a simplified small-size version of the logo, NOT a different character. Remove all words, lettering, shoulders, hand and outer shield. Make head/visor and the cyan halo centered, large bold silhouette filling 82% of a square canvas, readable at 32px. Clean shapes with much less microdetail, cyan visor the unmistakable focal point. Genuine transparent background beyond the halo/face silhouette. No square background, no frame, no extra symbol, no text, no mockup. Restrained close cyan rim glow only, no diffuse haze. Same branding style as the full supplied logo.

Reference: public/brand/set-free-logo.png.

## Website integration

The full logo appears first in the hero. A soft radial glow fades to transparency behind it, and a small floating animation pauses offscreen and when the tab is hidden. Reduced-motion visitors receive a static logo and Matrix background. The compact mark is used in the navbar and favicon variants. Next.js ImageResponse creates branded Open Graph and Twitter cards for the homepage and seven project pages.
